# Knight Character Art Style & Design Language

## Asset Description
The Knight is a frontline tank character. She wears heavy plate armor with blue and silver accents. She carries a large shield and a longsword.

## Art Style Guidelines
- **Perspective**: 2D Isometric (30 degrees).
- **Resolution**: 128x128.
- **Aesthetic**: Cute anime female knight. Blue and silver plate armor.
- **Background**: Solid magenta (#FF00FF).
- **Lighting**: Top-left directional light, flat drop shadows.
