# Wizard Character Art Style & Design Language

## Asset Description
The Wizard is a glass cannon magic user. She wears purple robes with gold trimmings and a large wizard hat. She carries a magical staff.

## Art Style Guidelines
- **Perspective**: 2D Isometric (30 degrees).
- **Resolution**: 128x128.
- **Aesthetic**: Cute anime female wizard. Purple and gold robes.
- **Background**: Solid magenta (#FF00FF).
- **Lighting**: Top-left directional light, flat drop shadows.
