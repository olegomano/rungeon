# Archer Character Art Style & Design Language

## Asset Description
The Archer is a ranged DPS character. She wears light leather armor with green and brown accents. She carries a recurve bow.

## Art Style Guidelines
- **Perspective**: 2D Isometric (30 degrees).
- **Resolution**: 128x128.
- **Aesthetic**: Cute anime female archer. Green and brown leather armor.
- **Background**: Solid magenta (#FF00FF).
- **Lighting**: Top-left directional light, flat drop shadows.
